SideQuest Arcade - play CrazyGames on top of World of Warcraft (macOS 13+, Apple Silicon or Intel)

1. Drag "SideQuest.app" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app (it isn't notarized yet):
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway", confirm.
   You only do this once.
3. In WoW: Options > Graphics > Display Mode = "Windowed (Fullscreen)".
   (Plain "Fullscreen" hides every overlay, including this one.)
4. A small game screen appears docked on the right, with a controller icon in the menu bar.

Hotkeys (they work while you're in WoW):
  Control+Option+Space   swap the keyboard: game <-> WoW (purple line = the game has the keyboard)
  Control+Option+G       show / hide the screen
  Control+Option+Left / Right   dock left / right
  Control+Option+= / -   bigger / smaller
  Control+Option+1..9    pick a game
  Control+Option+B       browse all of CrazyGames
  Control+Option+M       mute       Control+Option+R  reload

Clicking the game also gives it the keyboard; clicking WoW gives it back.
Menu bar icon: opacity and Quit. Drag the top bar to move it, drag the edges to resize.
